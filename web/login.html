Forgot your password?

The planner cannot send reset emails. Your church's admin resets passwords, so nothing about your account leaves the parish.

  1. Contact your church's admin: the music ministry lead, or whoever gave you your account.
  2. Tell them your username. They reset it on the Users page and give you a temporary password in person or by message.
  3. Sign in with the temporary password. The planner then asks you to choose a new one of at least 10 characters.

Locked out after five wrong tries? Wait 15 minutes, or ask the admin to unlock your account.

An admin who forgets their own password asks another admin of the church to reset it.
